## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The design tokens are unmistakably brand-authored — forest green #1f351c, burnt orange #cd4e0a, warm neutrals, Urbanist, "Serenity in every stroke," peso pricing, real salon services. But the My Bookings page itself is a near-generic booking-list card UI any hotel/clinic/gym could ship unchanged. The only salon signals are the design-name row and the ₱ sign. The join selects `design_image` (Booking.php:26) but never renders it — the one recognizable, delightful nail-art asset is thrown away. Header copy "View and manage your appointments" is neutral admin-speak.

**Deterministic scan:** detect.mjs exit 0, `[]` — zero findings on my-bookings.php + layout.php.

**Visual evidence:** Browser inspection (desktop 1280x800, mobile 390x844, empty state, live cancel run) confirms the rendered states and the findings below. Console clean, no 404s, no horizontal scroll.

## Overall Impression

A calm, coherent, genuinely well-styled booking list that holds up on mobile — undermined by two moments: an opaque, identity-less, OS-styled cancel flow whose only success feedback vanishes in 800ms, and a list that de-emphasizes the one column that matters (status) while shouting the least-relevant one (price). The bones are good; the emotional and informational edges are not.

## What's Working

1. Coherent, restrained visual system: tokens, banner, pills, card grid — verified clean on desktop and mobile.
2. Empty state is a real design decision: friendly copy, correct CTA, centered — verified on a fresh account.
3. Backend integrity behind cancel: ownership → 404, status guard → 400, notification email, button disables during flight.

## Priority Issues

- **P0 — Success feedback for an irreversible action is effectively invisible.** Toast fires then full-page reload wipes it in ~800ms (js/app.js:349-353); reload also loses scroll. *Fix:* update the card in place after success (flip pill, remove button) or persist a flash across reload.
- **P1 — The cancel confirmation doesn't say what it's cancelling.** Native confirm() names no booking (js/app.js:329). *Fix:* styled modal naming the booking ("Cancel Acrylic Full Set, Sat Aug 15, 1:00 PM?") and its consequence.
- **P1 — Error messaging is opaque and self-defeating.** Non-OK responses throw generic "Request failed," discarding the server's real message (js/app.js:344-345 vs BookingController.php:230). *Fix:* surface data.error; hide Cancel on terminal states.
- **P1 — Cancelled/confirmed bookings route to a "Booking Confirmed!" page.** The Details link points every booking at views/booking/confirmation.php whose header/subtitle hardcode "Booking Confirmed! / Your appointment has been scheduled" regardless of status. *Fix:* status-aware confirmation view (or a proper details page).
- **P2 — Destructive action styled as neutral.** Cancel is a small outline button beside a white Details button — near-identical on mobile (my-bookings.php:71-75). *Fix:* warning color/iconography or a separated destructive affordance.
- **P2 — List doesn't scale; status de-emphasized.** Date-only ordering, no filters/grouping/pagination (Booking.php:30); status is 11.5px pill under 20px bold orange price. *Fix:* filter tabs (Upcoming/Past/Cancelled) + rebalance price vs status weight.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex (power user):** no search/filter/sort/accelerators; scan cost grows per booking; ref "#9" and dead footer links are noise; every cancel re-finds the card after reload.
- **Sam (screen reader/keyboard):** H1→H3 heading skip; confirm dialog never announces which booking; role=status toast destroyed by reload before reliably announced; mobile menu role="dialog" lacks focus trap, Escape, aria-modal.
- **Casey (distracted mobile):** Cancel and Details are near-identical small buttons (fat-finger risk, no undo); confirm dialog can't be verified against the right appointment; success flash gone before they look back.

## Minor Observations

- Footer Services links are dead href="#" (layout.php:116-119).
- design_image selected (Booking.php:26) but never rendered in the card.
- Price is a single derived sum with no service/design breakdown (my-bookings.php:69).
- Cancel button label authored in two places (JS textContent vs CSS uppercase) — can flicker between casings.
- Past-dated pending booking still offers Cancel; no date guard (BookingController.php:229).

## Questions to Consider

1. Why is the one emotionally loaded moment handled by the browser's starkest dialog, when the brand promises "Serenity in every stroke"?
2. Why is cost the loudest element when the user's real question is "what's my status and when?"
3. Does "My Bookings" assume a customer books once? Nothing anticipates ten or thirty bookings.
4. What would the cancel moment look like designed with the same care as the header banner?
